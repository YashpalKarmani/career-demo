Our Story

Our Journey

From a simple idea to empowering thousands of career journeys

2022
2022 - The Beginning

A Real Problem

Careerly began when we witnessed countless bright students especially in Pakistan facing real career confusion. Some couldn't afford expensive career counseling. Others received outdated advice that didn't match today's job markets. Many felt lost choosing between dozens of fields without understanding what truly fit them. We knew there had to be a better way.

2023
2023 - Building with Purpose

Building with Purpose

We started by listening to students, fresh graduates, career coaches, and industry professionals.
